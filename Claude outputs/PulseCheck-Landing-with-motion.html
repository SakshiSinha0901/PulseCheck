Questions

Things people ask before signing up

How often do you check my website?

Every 5 minutes, all day. If a check fails, we mark the site as down.

How will I know if it goes down?

We send an email to the address you choose, when your site goes down and again when it comes back up.

Does it cost anything?

No. PulseCheck is free to start and you do not need a credit card.

What do you do with my email address?

We use it only to send you alerts about your websites. You can read the full policy on the Privacy page.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, at any time from Settings. Your websites and their check history are deleted with it.
